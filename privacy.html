Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Overview

Diagnology is an offline-first app. We collect no personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. There are no servers to send data to, no analytics SDKs to track behavior, and no login or account system that would require personal information.

What we do not collect

Diagnology does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Patient data: The app helps you look up diagnosis codes, but it does not see, store, or send any actual patient information.
  • Personal identification: No name, email, phone number, or account credentials are requested or stored.
  • Search history: The codes you look up are not logged to a server or sent anywhere.
  • Device identifiers: No IDFA (iOS), Android Advertising ID, or other unique device identifiers are collected.
  • Analytics: No third-party analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.) are embedded in the app.
  • Crash reports: Exception logs are not automatically sent to external services.
  • Location data: Your location is not requested or used.

How favorites are stored

When you save a code as a favorite with a personal note, that data is stored on your device only, in the app's local database. It is never uploaded to any server. If you uninstall the app, your favorites are deleted along with it. If you upgrade to a future paid tier (Phase 2), the app will migrate your local favorites safely to the upgraded version on the same device.

Offline operation

Diagnology is designed to run offline. The ICD-10-CM code table is bundled with the app when you download it. The app does not require an internet connection to look up codes. If you have internet enabled on your device, the app does not use it—the bundled table is complete and sufficient.

Code table updates

The app's code table is updated annually to match the US CMS fiscal-year release. When a new version of Diagnology ships with an updated code table, you download and install it normally through the App Store or Google Play. The code table is not updated over-the-air or automatically. You always know when the table is refreshed because you actively update the app.

Third-party libraries

Diagnology is built with no external dependencies beyond the platform's own standard library (Foundation on iOS, Android Framework on Android). There are no third-party SDKs bundled in the app.

Apple App Privacy Label

The App Store privacy label for Diagnology states: Data Not Collected. Apple's label-generating system confirms that the app has no code sections marked as data collection, and our submitted privacy information declares no personal data categories.

Google Play Data Safety

The Google Play Data Safety form for Diagnology answers "No" or "Not applicable" to all data collection questions. The app does not collect personal or sensitive information.

Contacting us about privacy

If you have questions about this privacy policy or Diagnology's data practices, please visit our support page for contact information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "last modified" date. Continued use of the app after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

Summary in one sentence: Diagnology never collects your personal data, never sends your lookups anywhere, and runs completely offline on your device.
