Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Who we are

Diagnology is published by 8086 LLC. Questions about this policy or your data: support@8086.llc.

Overview

Diagnology works offline. It has no account, no login, no server and no analytics. We collect no personal data: nothing you type or save in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app does not collect

  • Patient data: the app looks up diagnosis codes; it never asks for, stores or sends patient information.
  • Personal details: no name, email, phone number or account.
  • Search history: your searches stay in the app and are not sent anywhere.
  • Identifiers: no advertising ID or other device identifier is read.
  • Analytics and crash reports: no analytics or crash-reporting library is in the app, and nothing is sent automatically.
  • Location, contacts, photos: never requested.

What stays on your device

The favorites you save, the notes you write on them and your display settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device. They are included in your device's own backup if you use one (iCloud or Finder/iTunes backup on iPhone and iPad, Google's device backup on Android), under your account with Apple or Google, and they come back if you restore that backup. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them from the device; to remove them from a backup, delete that backup in your device's settings.

Network

The ICD-10-CM code table ships inside the app. The app makes no network requests; on Android it does not hold the internet permission. A new code table arrives only as a normal app update from the App Store or Google Play.

Libraries in the app

On iPhone and iPad the app uses only Apple's frameworks. On Android it uses Google's AndroidX libraries and protobuf-lite. None of them collects or sends data in Diagnology.

Store privacy answers

The App Store privacy label we submit declares Data Not Collected, and our Google Play Data safety form declares that no data is collected or shared. Both match this page.

Children

Diagnology is a reference for coding professionals and is not directed to children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will change first, with a new date at the top.
