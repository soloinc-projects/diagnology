Frequently Asked Questions

Is my search history stored?

No. Diagnology is offline-first and stores no search history to any server. The codes you look up stay on your device only. They are visible only to you, locally, until you close the app.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes, completely. The entire FY2027 ICD-10-CM code table (98,403 codes) is bundled with the app when you download it. You do not need internet to look up codes or use any feature of the app.

How often is the code table updated?

The code table is updated annually to match the US CMS fiscal-year release. When a new code table is available, we release a new version of the app. You update it normally through the App Store or Google Play. There are no over-the-air updates to code data.

What happens to my favorites if I upgrade to the paid tier?

When we release Phase 2 (the paid tier), your existing favorites and notes will be migrated safely to the upgraded version on the same device. You will not lose your data.

Can I export my favorites?

This feature is planned for a future release. For now, favorites are stored locally in the app and can be viewed within the app only.

Does the app include laterality and 7th-character guidance?

The app displays the primary code and its billability flag. Laterality and 7th-character guidance (win-list items 6 and 4 in our roadmap) are coming in a future release and will require a secondary data pull from the CMS Tabular List.

What is your refund policy?

Refunds are handled by Apple (iOS) and Google (Android) according to their app store policies. Contact the app store directly if you purchased the app and want a refund.

Is the app accessible for users with vision or hearing disabilities?

Yes. Diagnology is built with accessibility in mind and meets WCAG 2.2 AA standards. The app works with VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android) screen readers, supports text scaling at the largest device setting, and works in both light and dark modes.

Report a bug or request a feature

If you've found a bug or have a feature request, please email us at support@8086.llc with:

  • Your device and OS version (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 26.0)
  • The code table effective date shown in the app
  • A clear description of what happened
  • Steps to reproduce, if applicable

Contact support
